MorningAfter Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

MorningAfter is an Android app designed to help users reduce distracting app usage after waking up. This privacy policy explains what information MorningAfter accesses, how that information is used, and what third-party services may process information while you use the app.

Health Connect and sleep data

MorningAfter can access sleep data through Android Health Connect when you grant permission. MorningAfter uses this information to determine when you have finished sleeping so that a configured MorningAfter lock can begin.

Health Connect data is used only for MorningAfter's wake-detection and locking features. MorningAfter does not use Health Connect data for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

MorningAfter does not sell Health Connect data or share it with advertisers.

Accessibility Service

MorningAfter uses an Android Accessibility Service during an active lock. The service is used to detect when a selected app or certain protected Android Settings pages are opened so that MorningAfter can display its blocking screen.

Accessibility information is used only to provide MorningAfter's app-blocking and lock-protection features. MorningAfter does not use accessibility data for advertising, user profiling, or analytics.

App preferences and activity logs

MorningAfter stores configuration information on your device, such as your selected apps, lock duration, wake-monitoring settings, and MorningAfter activity log.

This information is used to operate the app and remains stored locally on your device unless otherwise stated in this policy.

Advertising

MorningAfter may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostic information in order to provide, measure, and protect advertising services.

Where required, MorningAfter uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request or manage advertising consent before requesting ads.

You can learn more about how Google processes information through its advertising services here:

Google Privacy Policy

Accounts

MorningAfter does not require you to create a MorningAfter account.

Data sales

MorningAfter does not sell your personal information.

Children's privacy

MorningAfter is not specifically directed toward children under 13. MorningAfter does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a MorningAfter account or developer-operated server.

Your choices

You can revoke permissions granted to MorningAfter through Android or Health Connect settings. You can also change or disable MorningAfter's locking and monitoring features within the app when those controls are available.

If advertising privacy options are required for your region, MorningAfter provides access to Google's privacy options interface.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated as MorningAfter changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
